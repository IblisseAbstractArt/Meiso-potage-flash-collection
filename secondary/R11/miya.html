プロダクトゲーム Information Game

=====================================================

Kijin Seija's Reversed Puzzle Dice

=====================================================

Hello.

I am Mi~ya from Meisou Potage.
Thank you very much for picking up "Miso-aji". It is our usual hand-burned CD distribution, but we made things with all our might (within a limited time).

This time, it's a "falling-block puzzle" featuring the currently popular (?) Seija-chan. I was thinking about whether I could make an interesting game using Seija's "flip" ability, and it suddenly occurred to me: why not tie it in with "flipping dice"...
(This is probably influenced by recently getting into TRPGs... lol, kind of associated from Sword World's fate alteration).
...And so.

What you see here is something I forcefully tied together. It really is forced!
This kind of falling-block puzzle is probably better as a versus mode, isn't it... This time, it's a single-player game where you simply aim for a high score.
Chains are hard to aim for, so it's a bit plain, but screen flips occur frequently in the late game, which might make that part a bit fun?
Anyway, I would be happy if you could play it just to kill a little time.

## Play Hints


* Use blocks sandwiched in between as a starting point to clear them all at once like a fuse.

* The mysterious meter in the bottom right is the balance meter (tentative). If you sandwich smaller numbers (6-1-6, 5-2-5, 4-3-4), the needle shifts to the right; if you sandwich larger numbers (1-6-1, 2-5-2, 3-4-3), the needle shifts to the left.

* The more skewed the balance meter becomes, the easier it moves in the direction of further skewing. Conversely, the force returning it to $\pm0$ becomes weaker.

* To be completely honest, the author himself hasn't grasped the optimal strategy.
